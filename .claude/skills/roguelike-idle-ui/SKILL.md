# Roguelike idle UI (Gear Defenders look)

Three layers, each with one job:

1. **Art = SVG** in `game/ui/skin/`, generated by `tools/ui/make_svgs.py` from the measured
   palette ([reference/measurements.md](reference/measurements.md)). No Gear Defenders or
   GearPaw files are used — shapes are rebuilt from measurements.
2. **Skin = Godot Theme** `game/ui/skin/game_theme.tres`, built by `tools/ui/build_theme.gd`:
   9-slice `StyleBoxTexture`s for buttons, panels, cards, bars; fonts and label styles.
   Looks are chosen with `theme_type_variation` (list at the top of `build_theme.gd`).
3. **Layout = Controls + Containers.** `game/ui/Ui.gd` builds the pieces (HUD, plaques,
   cards, cost buttons, bottom nav…). No hard-coded drawing coordinates; GDScript only for
   motion (bar fill, glow, press shift).

Reference screens: `tests/ui/LobbyPreview.tscn` (cat/troops screen), `tests/ui/GearsPreview.tscn`
(gear inventory, includes a locked button). Copy their structure for new screens.

## Pipeline (after changing art)

```powershell
python -I tools/ui/make_svgs.py                                   # 1. regenerate SVGs
& $godot --headless --path . --import                             # 2. import new/changed SVGs
& $godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/ui/build_theme.gd   # 3. rebuild the theme
& $godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/ui/preview_skin.gd -- C:\tmp\skin.png  # optional: all SVGs on one sheet
```
`$godot = "C:\Users\dangp\OneDrive\Desktop\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe"` (not on PATH).
Hand edits to a generated SVG are lost on the next `make_svgs.py` run — change the script.

## Buttons: our own look, not a Gear Defenders copy

`button()` in make_svgs.py: one shared dark-brown outline `#583f21` (same line as the cats) for
every tone, a **flat single-colour face** on a deeper lip band (from the cat-game `ui 1`
slabs), soft rounded corners (r 26 of 128), and a faint **paw print in the top-right corner**
as the signature — kept inside the unstretched 9-slice corner (theme margins 40/33/40/36).
Do **not** bring back GD's `bth_*` traits: per-tone outline, light/dull split at half height,
inner light rim, end glints. The `bth_*` numbers in measurements.md are reference only.

## Nav and item cards: our own look (second round of feedback)

- **Bottom nav:** unselected tabs are light grey tiles (`nav_cell`, #cfd3d8; dark slate muddied the icon outlines) with icons ~100 px that nearly
  fill them; the selected tab is a warm orange→gold block with faint paw prints (`nav_selected`)
  and a 116 px icon rising above it. Do not use GD's blue block with mountains.
- **Item cards** (`Ui.gear_card`): keep the rarity card frame (CardUnit); the item art fills
  ~84% of it. When feedback says an item looks "too simple", redraw the **item art** with more
  detail — do not change the card frame (the `gear_bg_*` frames were rejected for this).
- Every icon part gets a **rim light** (white edge along its top-left, from the `Icon` class) so
  items read as volume, not flat cut-outs.

## Icon strokes (the `Icon` class in make_svgs.py)

Measured on the Gear Defenders icons (coinIcon/gemIcon 72 px, aixin_b 146, gear4 123): outline
3–4 **screen** px at every size from 72 up, sitting **outside** the shape, a little heavier at
the bottom; inner edges thinner than the silhouette. Rules the class enforces:
- **Outside stroke:** each part is stroked at 2× width *under* its fill, so fills keep full
  size and thin parts (handles, bow limbs) survive. Never stroke on top of a fill.
- **One silhouette:** all part outlines are drawn first, then all fills; overlapping parts are
  separated only by a hairline inner edge (`INNER_PX` ≈ 1.1 px). Don't add per-part heavy lines.
- **Constant-pixel weight with optical sizing:** icons are generated per display size
  (`ICON_SIZES` 32/48/64/96/128; files `icon_x_32.svg`…, plain `icon_x.svg` = 128).
  `outline_px(size)` = 5.5% of the size, clamped to 2–4 px — a 3.5 px line on a 32 px icon
  turned a bow into a dark blob. Lip and drop shadow are a share of the icon size, not of the
  line. `Ui.icon(name, size)` / `Ui.icon_tex` pick the variant ≥ the shown size: **always
  load icons through them**, never a fixed file.
- Open strokes (X, +, chevron, padlock shackle) use `Icon.line(d, width, color)` so they get
  the same outline. One outline colour for all icons (`ICON_LINE` #3c1e0c).
- Thin parts must stay ≥ ~10 units wide in the 128 grid.
- Bottom-nav tabs use illustrated scene icons, never reused currency icons, rendered **flat in the
  cat style** (`Icon.flat_svg`, user rule) so they match the hero-cat head tab: `tab_shop`
  (striped stall), `tab_cat` (the hero cat head in the flat cat style — cats never get the UI
  icon style), `tab_battle` (two big crossed swords, no backing), `tab_gear` (meshing gears),
  `tab_summon` (crystal ball), `tab_bag` (loot sack).
- HUD: **no background band** — avatar (in its frame), resource pills and EXP bar sit straight
  on each screen's own background (sky, wall, board), like both references. "Background
  trắng" in feedback meant this band, not the avatar frame.
- HUD resources (`Ui._currency`): equal-width pills (SIZE_EXPAND_FILL) of fixed height 36 with a
  full radius, icon (48) overlapping the pill's left end, value centred, green "+" inside the
  right end, timer tag under the energy pill. Anything placed *on* a pill goes on the plain
  Control parent: a PanelContainer re-lays out its children and ignores pinned offsets.
- Badges are icons too: `badge_shield_blue/orange`, `badge_check`, `badge_dot`, `badge_plus`,
  `badge_flower` — load them with `Ui.icon`, never draw a badge with its own stroke style.
- Check with `tools/ui/preview_icon_sizes.gd` (rows: old downscale | 32 | 48 | 64 | 128).

## Layout rules (from the real Gear Defenders screens)

1. **Three depth layers.** Back: the scene (castle wall, field) or a muted board/blue panel.
   Middle: saturated cards and buttons. Top: white text with a dark outline.
2. **Group with sunken wells under a plaque title** (`Well` + `title_plaque`, or a blue panel
   with `section_plaque` sitting on its top edge). No frames inside frames.
3. **One card anatomy everywhere:** level badge top-left, art in the middle, name or a
   `have/need` bar at the bottom, a green check when in use. Rarity = card colour.
4. **One main action per area.** Small pills for secondary actions; big two-line cost
   buttons along the bottom thumb zone.
5. **Compact HUD** (`Ui.hud`) on top, `Ui.bottom_nav` at the bottom.
6. **Every button has normal / pressed / locked** (the Figma brief asks for all three).
7. Screen 720×1280. Side margins 12–24 px, grid gaps 10–18 px, card text 15–18 px, titles
   22–30 px, primary buttons ≥ 60 px tall (touch target).

## Review checklist (from user feedback on the previews)

Look for these before showing a screen:
- **Centre subject truly centred.** The art itself must be symmetric about its centre (trees
  mirrored, no one-sided props) and characters on it placed at `-width/2`. A centred frame
  around off-centre art still reads as "lệch".
- **Related bits form one centred group.** Wave marks and the reward chest are one track
  (1 ✓ — 2 ✓ — 3 — chest, gold links for done) with the caption centred under it — not a
  separate left-aligned "icon + text" row.
- **No dead bands.** The column must fill the height on any screen: the centre subject grows
  into the leftover space (wrap it in an `AspectRatioContainer` inside an EXPAND_FILL margin,
  and anchor characters on it in fractions), the info under it and the CTA row follow
  directly; side items go lower down too (e.g. "Tăng tốc" / "Triệu hồi" beside the track) so
  the sides are not empty. Never place the CTA or locked stages with fixed offsets from a
  corner — put them in the column's CTA row. Reference: `tests/ui/HomePreview.gd`
  `_build_center`.
- **A stat and its action are one block** (cost button inside the strip), with clear space
  between the strip and characters/stands below; nothing overlaps section plaques.
- **HUD tidy:** equal pills, icons attached to pills, no floating text.
- **Bottom nav like the references:** icons nearly fill each tab (84 px unselected, 100 px
  selected rising above its block), **no labels on unselected tabs**, only the selected tab
  shows its name.
- **Every feature icon is drawn for its name** (`rail_pass` winged crest, `rail_vip` VIP card,
  `rail_limited` gift + clock, `rail_quest` scroll, `rail_online` hourglass, `rail_checkin`
  calendar tick); rail icons ~80 px. Never reuse star/gem/coin for a feature.
- **Stage art is real isometric:** rhombus top, lit left / dark right dirt faces, grass lip,
  trees at the back, bushes and stones at the front (`stage_island` in make_svgs.py). A flat
  front-on block reads as weak.
- **Icons not monotone:** nav tabs are illustrated; don't reuse currency icons for navigation.
- Change only what the feedback names (e.g. "icon dưới đơn điệu" = the tab icons, not the bar).

## Gotchas

- Godot's SVG import rasterises at the file's size: author art at (or above) the size it is
  shown, and keep `texture_filter = LINEAR` on the screen root (`Ui.setup_root`) — the
  project default is nearest for the pixel-art packs.
- SVG features to avoid: filters (blur/drop-shadow) and text. Shadows are separate shapes.
- Badges on cards must not be children of a `PanelContainer` (it stretches them): use a
  plain `Control` root with the panel full-rect and badges placed with `Ui.pin`.
- A 9-slice keeps its corners; keep plaques at their art height (stretching the pointed ends
  vertically distorts them).
- `var x := array[0]` / `:=` from untyped calls fails to parse; type it or use `=`.
- A parse error leaves the Godot window open; `shot.ps1` stops it after a timeout. Only stop
  the process you started — others may be the user's editor.
- Cats use a different, flat style: follow the `cat-sprite-art` skill, not these UI rules.
- Item art (chests, weapons) in the references is hand-drawn; the code icons are simplified.
  Say so when reporting.
- No files are copied, but the look is very close to Gear Defenders: fine for the demo and
  coursework; for a public release, shift palette/shapes to give the game its own identity.

## Popups and the Sự kiện hub
- Every popup goes through `UiKit.sheet_popup` (UiKit.popup / dark_popup route there): dark
  scrim, cream `Sheet` window, `title_plaque` sitting on its top edge (VBox separation -30,
  plaque row z_index 1), red round close on the top-right corner. Child 0 of the returned box
  is a spacer, kept because old callers skip index 0.
- Sự kiện = pattern UI-068: one purple banner for the featured event (hero on a glow, name,
  countdown `Ui.counter`, yellow cost button "Triệu hồi / Miễn phí" pulsing + red dot when
  free, "Nổi bật" tag on the corner), then "Sự kiện khác" rows in `Well`s with fixed columns:
  drawn icon 84 (rail_patrol, rail_checkin, rail_quest) | name + progress (value bar or 7 day
  marks) | one button 150x68 — green "Nhận/Thu" when something waits (plus red dot on the
  icon), blue "Đi/Xem" otherwise.
- Touch targets: anything laid over a window (close X, badges you tap) must come **after** the window in the tree — a later PanelContainer swallows taps even where an earlier sibling is drawn on top (z_index does not change input order). Close X is 68 px on the window corner; a tap on the scrim closes the popup too. Check with tests/PopupCloseTest.tscn.
- CheckBox: no button face; ticked = badge_check 40, empty = box_empty 40.
- Overlays on their own CanvasLayer (ChestOpening, SummonOpening, admin) do NOT inherit the lobby's LINEAR filter: set `texture_filter = LINEAR` on their root or scaled-up SVG art looks jagged ("răng cưa"). For art shown large, load the 128 icon variant (e.g. `UiKit.coin_texture(128)`).
- Cards with a fixed size (summon/chest reveal 122×168): budget the content height (label + art + 2-line name + tag ≤ inner height) or it spills out of the card bottom.
- Contrast (checked 2026-10-18, WCAG): LabelDark is #5c3d38 (≥4.8:1 on Well/Board). White/colour text relies on its dark outline (#2e1c1c, ~12:1); never put colour-coded text on a card of a similar hue (accent green on green cards was 1.25:1, use white). Gold icon parts need a deeper backing: the selected nav block is orange #f07b2a→#d9562a, not gold.
- Selected nav tab: the icon (106) sits inside the block. An icon rising over the block's top edge tangled the two outlines ("stroke hơi loạn").
- Card backs: `UiKit.card_back_face(size, summon)` — inset light frame + one centred medallion (paw / crystal ball), anchored children. A big "?" label stacked in a PanelContainer drifted with font metrics.
- SVG outlines are centred on their path: a path on the canvas edge loses half its stroke there (sides looked thinner than the top on nav_selected). Inset edge paths by half the stroke width. The selected nav tab is 1.3× wider than the others (size_flags_stretch_ratio).
- Nav icons (user references, 2026-10-18): Chiến = two CHUNKY crossed swords (broad white round-tipped blades, big curved gold guards — "béo vào" meant fatter, not slimmer); Lính = a fat shield with a thick gold rim and a red field in four diagonal facets. Both in the cat style.
