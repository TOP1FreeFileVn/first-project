# Shop sections and gem packs — native review

The serialized production `Home.tscn` loads the ordinary `ShopPage.gd`; no draft page or injected art is used. An isolated account under `output/shop-sections-profile` runs with `PlayerData.persist = false`.

Run from the repository root:

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File docs/ui_review/2026-10-24/shop-sections/run.ps1
```

The fixture captures 360×640 and 450×800 views of the shop top, supply section, gem section top, last pack row, first/last pack detail sheets, and gold offers before/after the daily claim. It checks centered section placards, Gold ×1/×10 and existing Wood/Silver controls, six gem packs in three columns and two rows, a matching gold grid, scroll reachability, and the preserved original bottom navigation.

Real pointer input selects and dismisses the first and last proposed pack at both sizes. The amount and price must match the catalog; the sheet must explain that payment has not been integrated. A full player snapshot must remain identical through selecting and dismissing each pack, including currency and inventory.

Pack amounts and VND prices are design proposals until a real store catalog and verified billing are connected. Selecting a pack does not charge money or award diamonds.

`manifest.json` and native PNG captures are generated by a successful run. The runner requires the explicit completion marker with an empty failure list, so a script parse error with a zero process exit cannot appear to pass.

The gem cards use six individual generated illustrations on a layered lilac backdrop, with broad translucent facets and a soft pearl light. Purple now covers the full inner face including the header; only the narrow outer rim stays cream. Each 512×512 asset has its meaningful painted bounds centered on both axes; the 180-unit illustration area preserves that alignment for short and tall objects alike.

The gold section contains 500 free gold per local calendar day and five diamond exchanges, arranged in three columns. Exchange sizes 1/5/10/25/50 preserve the configured base rate (currently 20 diamonds → 500 gold), with no extra bonus. The daily claim day is saved under `shop.daily_gold_day`; repeat claims and clock rollback to an earlier day cannot reopen the gift. Real pointer checks verify the daily grant and first/last exchanges, the shared currency header, reload/rebuild blocking, and unaffordable actions. The focused `tests/ShopGoldTest.tscn` suite covers all tiers, invalid requests, changed admin rates, daily rollover, and older saves.

Silver supplies have a cool pearl/blue backdrop, while wood keeps its cream backdrop. Both use the same quiet rounded card shape, original button artwork, shared brown contour and absence of an external panel shadow. Silver reward copy uses two intentional centered lines so “đá rèn” stays together. Visual tier detail belongs to the chest illustration itself, rather than ornamenting the surrounding UI frame.

The gold cards now use six separately generated illustrations paired with the gem concepts: a small cluster, pouch, tray, carton box, open chest and overflowing chest. Purple gems become bright yellow gear-stamped coins while the container compositions remain. Their generated alpha and colors are preserved in centered 512x512 assets under `game/ui/art/shop_gold_packs/`. The native fixture verifies each offer's assigned texture, unique decoded artwork, centered painted bounds and containment inside its card.

Latest native review passed 507 checks and 18 captures at both viewport sizes, including a live rate update without rebuilding the offer. Earlier ShopTeal regression passed 127 checks and 7 captures; the focused gold backend suite passed 78 checks. The gold art installer independently verified six distinct centered RGBA assets and reproducible outputs. Native results and capture count are recorded in `manifest.json`.
