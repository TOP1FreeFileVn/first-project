# Summon icon proposals

Demo assets only. Both SVGs use a 128-unit viewBox, 512-pixel output size, flat material fills and the game's warm-brown main contour. Review at 26 px in a closed carton and 44 px in an open carton.

- **A — Cat portal (`art/summon-portal.svg`, recommended):** a lavender gateway with cat-ear shoulders, a broad turquoise opening and a pale door plane. One small sparkle suggests magic. The tall open silhouette communicates entry and summoning, and stays distinct from the hero face, troops shield and shop.
- **B — Summon invitation (`art/summon-invitation.svg`):** a purple invitation emerging from a large cream envelope, secured with a coral cat-shaped wax seal. It is a warmer, more collectible direction; the envelope may also read as mail, so it needs the existing “Triệu hồi” caption for context.

The shared main outline is 6.5 units with rounded joins. Minor construction marks use quieter material-colored ink; colored planes and the portal opening do not each receive a heavy frame. Neither option repeats the existing crystal ball, adds gradients/glow, or illustrates the four troop systems.

Production icons, CartonNav and asset imports remain unchanged.

Validation: both SVGs loaded successfully through Godot's native SVG rasterizer at 512 × 512. The 26/44 px review sheet is `output/summon-menu-small-review.png` (previous icon, A, B from left to right).
