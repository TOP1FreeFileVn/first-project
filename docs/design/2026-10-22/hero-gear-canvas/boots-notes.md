# Boot proposal

A pair of upright side-profile steel boots replaces the single-boot proposal, per the user's correction. Both toes point left; the front boot sits lower and hides the rear boot's overlapping foot edge. Both cuffs stay readable. The boot proportions were redrawn for the paired composition instead of scaling two already-stroked icons down. Original single-boot geometry remains in `boots-single.js`, with its review in `review-single.png`.

The rear heels stay nearly vertical while broad toes project left. Tall ankles, open cuffs and curved toe-cap seams explain the item without buckles, jewels or decorative seams. Only the same two steel fills as the latest armor are used; soles are broad unlined material regions.

- Main: **11.35 source px**, warm brown `#583f21`, along the outer silhouette and exposed cuff opening.
- Secondary: **7.41 source px**, `#9b7f58`, for the lower shin and toe-cap construction seams, the only two construction lines. This carries the user's latest +50% secondary adjustment.
- Fill boundaries receive no extra thin gray stroke. Hidden/shared edges are drawn once.
- Upright, with no 45-degree rotation or per-boot scale. The pair uses translations only, keeping both line classes at their final intended widths. Front fill occludes rear outlines instead of doubling the shared visible edge.

This is a stat-only icon proposal; it is not composited onto the cat and does not overwrite production art.
