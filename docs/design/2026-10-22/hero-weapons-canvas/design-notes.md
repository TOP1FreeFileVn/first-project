# Staff and axe draft construction

The staff uses one shared vertical axis for the crystal, its collar and the wooden shaft. A broad crystal and a shallow collar replace the old small diamond attached to a long generic stick. Its two aqua planes are fill regions; the collar join and two grip construction marks are secondary strokes. The collar occludes the crystal base and shaft in one continuous outer silhouette.

The axe has a broad asymmetric bearded blade, a short poll and a readable undercut. The head sits over a smooth, unmarked wooden handle; only the exposed shaft edges are inked, so the junction is not a double outline. Two clean steel fill regions define the blade with no outlined facet or extra surface mark.

Both sources use local grip `(0, 0)` and the head towards `-Y`. The caller places and fits each weapon independently around the real paw socket. The default icon working profile is main `13` / secondary `6.5` local units. For held art, pass widths compensated for the caller's uniform scale to obtain main `9 px` and the chosen secondary target on the `256 × 240` hero canvas. This default is a draft working profile, not the helmet/armor/boots `11.35/7.41` profile or a universal ratio.

Main ink is `#583f21`; subordinate seams use the quieter warm `#9b7f58`. There is no decorative gray outline, gradient, glossy effect, cast shadow or global dilation. Keep broad fills visible after placement and inspect actual-size held views before integration. Production art is unchanged by these two source files.

## Axe second construction, after the user's redraw request

The first source is preserved as `axe-v1.js`. The replacement removes the square stepped poll/neck and angular thin hook. Its compact rounded poll flows into a broad curved head with one long smooth cutting arc, a shallow beard undercut and substantial silver fill. The wooden haft initially grew from 22 to 30 local units wide; its rounded butt is part of the haft, with no small boxed attachment. The latest correction below widens the grip further and removes the eye mark. The two broad silver planes have no separating stroke. Review with the caller's proposed right-paw pose `28° / 0.60 scale`, checking the head against the right brow before integration.

## Secondary-stroke revisions and final removal

The user's next correction exposed contact between the butt marks and the main contour. The haft tapers from the original 30-unit mounting to a 40-unit grip. The first clearance fix shortened the marks to x=±5 and made them read as dots; the user rejected that treatment. `round-5-long-wraps.png` records the subsequent longer wraps with narrow cap-aware gaps; `round-6-clean-head.png` removes the unnecessary steel-head mark. These are historical rounds. The latest user instruction removes both remaining grip wraps too: the current axe has a smooth, unmarked wooden handle and a clean two-fill steel head, shown in `round-7-clean-handle.png` and `round-7-axe-crop.png`. The source and export intentionally contain only main strokes, with no unused secondary profile or ink; the two stroke classes do not require adding unnecessary marks.
